# Math

Inline math such as $E = mc^2$ sits in a sentence. Scoring background: [[CVSS 3.1 metrics]], [[Severity and CVSS]], [[Risk rating overview]].

## Block formulas

A simple risk estimate multiplies likelihood and impact:

$$
R = L \times I
$$

The mean of $n$ scores is

$$
\bar{s} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} s_i
$$

## Entropy of a password

For a length $k$ and an alphabet of size $N$, the entropy is $H = k \log_2 N$ bits. With $k = 12$ and $N = 62$ this gives about $71.5$ bits.
