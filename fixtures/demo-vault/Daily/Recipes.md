## Lentil soup

Serves four, 35 minutes.

- 250 g red lentils
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 l vegetable stock
- 1 teaspoon cumin

1. Soften the onion and carrots for 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, lentils and stock.
3. Simmer for 20 minutes, then blend half.

> [!tip]
> A spoon of yogurt and some lemon juice lift the soup.
