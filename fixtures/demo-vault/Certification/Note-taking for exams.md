# Note-taking for exams

Notes taken during an exam become the report. Write them so that a stranger could reproduce your steps.

## What to record

- Target, time and what you did.
- The result, even when it is a failure.
- Exact commands with variables in place of secrets.
- Where the screenshot is saved, following [[Screenshot conventions]].

## Structure

One note per target, with the same headings every time:

```markdown
# Target A

## Summary
## Services
## Attempts
## Evidence
```

The method is the same as the one in [[Journal keeping]] and [[Evidence handling]], applied under time pressure.

## Rules

> [!note] Check the exam rules
> Some exams limit which tools or notes may be used. Read [[Exam rules recap]] and the vendor's page before relying on a plug-in or a template.

## After the day

Turn the notes into an [[Exam report]] and keep a copy in your study archive. Short entries on what went wrong belong in [[Lab log]].
