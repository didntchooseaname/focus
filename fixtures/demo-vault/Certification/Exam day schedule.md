# Exam day schedule

A template to adapt once the vendor's duration and rules are known. Hours below are an example for a long exam, not a fact about any specific one.

## Before the start

- [ ] Sleep, food and water prepared
- [ ] Machine updated the day before, not that morning
- [ ] Notes vault synced and opened, see [[Note-taking for exams]]
- [ ] Identity documents and proctoring tools ready, per the vendor's page
- [ ] [[Exam rules recap]] read once more

## Example timeline

| Block | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Hour 0 | Read the brief, list targets |
| Hours 1 to 4 | First pass over every target |
| Hour 5 | Break, short walk |
| Hours 6 to 12 | Deepen the most promising targets |
| Last hours | Gather evidence and outline the report |

> [!warning] Breaks count
> Skipping meals and sleep costs more time than it saves. Put breaks in the plan, as in [[Time management]].

## After the exam

Write the report from your notes and screenshots while the memory is fresh. Structure and checks are in [[Exam report]]. Log what you learned in [[Lab log]].
