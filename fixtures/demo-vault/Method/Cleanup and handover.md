# Cleanup and handover

At the end of testing we remove what we added and deliver what we owe.

## Cleanup

- [x] Remove test accounts we created
- [x] Remove files we placed on client systems
- [ ] Ask the client to revoke the test accounts they gave us
- [ ] Remove our source addresses from allow lists

Every item is checked against the [[Journal keeping]] note, since the journal lists what we created.

## Our own data

Client data is deleted from our machines 90 days after the report, unless the contract says otherwise. Keep the note of the deletion date in the [[Engagement lifecycle]] list.

> [!note]
> The archive is encrypted and sent by a channel agreed in [[Client communication]].
