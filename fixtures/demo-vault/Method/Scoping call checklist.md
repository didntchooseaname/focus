# Scoping call checklist

The scoping call lasts 45 minutes. Its output is a draft [[Scope table]] and a list of open questions.

## Before the call

- [x] Read the previous engagement notes if the client is known
- [x] Prepare the question list below
- [ ] Book a second slot in case the call overruns

## Questions

| Topic | What we need | Where it goes |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Targets | Domains, ranges, applications | [[Scope table]] |
| Windows | Allowed days and hours | [[Rules of engagement]] |
| Contacts | Technical and escalation | [[Client communication]] |
| Data | What may be touched or copied | [[Evidence handling]] |
| Fragile systems | Anything that must not be scanned | [[Out of scope discovery]] |

> [!question] What does the client fear most?
> Write the answer down in their words. It shapes the [[Executive summary]] later.

## After the call

Send the summary within one working day. State the dates, the targets as we understood them, and what stays unknown. The client answers by reply, and the answer is filed in the journal.
