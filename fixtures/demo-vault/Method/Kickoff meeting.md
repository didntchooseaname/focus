# Kickoff meeting

A 30 minute call on the first day of testing. Both teams confirm that the documents say what everybody thinks they say.

## Agenda

1. Read the [[Scope table]] aloud, row by row.
2. Confirm the window from the [[Rules of engagement]].
3. Exchange emergency numbers ([[Client communication]]).
4. Agree on the rhythm of [[Status updates]].
5. Ask what changed since scoping.

## Checklist

- [x] Authorization letter on file ([[Authorization letter]])
- [x] Test accounts received
- [ ] Source addresses whitelisted by the client
- [ ] Backup and restore contact named

