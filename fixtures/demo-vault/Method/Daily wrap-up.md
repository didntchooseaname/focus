# Daily wrap-up

Ten minutes at the end of each testing day. It turns the [[Journal keeping]] entries into three lines.

## Template

```markdown
## Wrap-up 2026-10-07

Done: service scan of S1, review of portal login.
Found: 2 candidate findings (not yet confirmed).
Tomorrow: check unchecked rows in the service inventory.
Hours: REC 2.5, TST 4.0, DOC 1.0
```

## Checklist

- [ ] Journal is up to date
- [ ] New evidence is hashed ([[Evidence handling]])
- [ ] Hours entered ([[Time tracking]])
- [ ] Status update drafted if due ([[Status updates]])
- [ ] Anything out of scope noted ([[Out of scope discovery]])

> [!note]
> The wrap-up is for us. It is not sent to the client as is.

## Weekly view

On Fridays the wrap-ups are read in order. The [[Weekly review]] collects what stayed open.
